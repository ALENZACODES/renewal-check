Before you sign

  1. Is this the best rate you can offer me, and what term is it for?
  2. Can I keep my current amortization, or are you changing it?
  3. What are my prepayment limits, and what is the penalty if I break the term?
  4. How many days do I have to accept, and can I hold this rate while I compare?
  5. Is the mortgage insured or uninsured? That affects how easily I can move it.

Switching lenders at renewal

Since November 21, 2024, OSFI no longer expects federally regulated lenders to apply the minimum qualifying rate (the stress test) when you move an uninsured mortgage to another federally regulated lender at renewal, as long as you don't add to the loan amount or the amortization. Lenders still check that you can afford it. OSFI: minimum qualifying rate for uninsured mortgages
